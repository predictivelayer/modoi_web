Nuclear Blocker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 Autonome

This policy describes how Nuclear Blocker (iPhone, iPad, and the companion Mac app) handles information. Nuclear Blocker is a self-control site blocker published by Autonome (People’s Republic of China). For company-wide privacy practices, read the Autonome Privacy Policy. We built Nuclear Blocker so your blocking choices stay on your devices and in your own Apple account, not on Autonome servers.

Summary

  • We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or ad tracking in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Block lists, timers, and schedules are stored locally on each device.
  • Optional sync uses your private iCloud (CloudKit). Modoi does not operate a backend that stores this data.
  • Blocking uses Apple system APIs (Screen Time on iOS, a network content filter on Mac). We do not receive your browsing history.

What Modoi does not collect

The apps do not send usage data, diagnostics, or browsing activity to Modoi. A search of the app source shows no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. Diagnostic messages written with Apple’s unified logging stay on your device unless you choose to share them (for example through a sysdiagnose or when you email us).

Information stored on your devices

To work, Nuclear Blocker stores data locally, including:

  • Website domains you chose to block (and related filter settings on Mac)
  • Manual block session start and end times, duration, and strict-mode flags
  • Recurring schedules (weekdays, time windows, names)
  • On Mac: license and trial state for the separate Mac purchase flow (stored in local app storage, not in a Modoi cloud)

On Mac, a small local web server on 127.0.0.1 may show the branded “blocked” page in your browser. It does not send traffic to Modoi.

On Mac, the network filter extension inspects connection hostnames against your block list on the device. It drops matching connections locally. It does not upload hostnames to us.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed into iCloud on your devices, Nuclear Blocker can sync a single shared state record through Apple’s CloudKit, in the app’s private iCloud container (iCloud.com.zachfu.NuclearBlocker). Synced fields include your block list, schedule definitions, and active session metadata (times and flags). This stays within your Apple ID’s private CloudKit database. Modoi does not have a server-side copy we can read.

Sync requires the same iCloud account on Mac and iOS/iPadOS. If iCloud or CloudKit is unavailable, the app keeps working with local data only.

Screen Time and system blocking (iOS / iPadOS)

On iPhone and iPad, blocking uses Apple’s Family Controls and Managed Settings APIs. You must grant Screen Time permission. The app applies shields to the website domains you listed. It does not read your full browsing history. Apple’s platform controls how shields behave across apps and browsers.

A Shield Configuration extension only customizes the appearance of the system block screen. It does not send data to Modoi.

Purchases

iPhone / iPad: Paid features, if any, are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that transaction. We do not receive your payment card number.

Mac: The Mac app is distributed separately. When you buy a Mac license, checkout is handled by Paddle as merchant of record in the default configuration (hosted checkout in your browser). Paddle’s privacy policy governs payment data. The Mac app stores license state locally. It does not send purchase details to Modoi unless you later add a server-side verification flow.

When you contact us

If you email support@modoi.ai, Autonome receives what you send (your address, message, and any attachments). We use it only to respond and improve support. We do not use support mail for marketing.

Children’s privacy

Nuclear Blocker is intended for adults who want self-imposed site blocking. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email support@modoi.ai and we will delete the correspondence.

No sale or sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use your data for cross-app tracking.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@modoi.ai.

Product help: see our Support page.
