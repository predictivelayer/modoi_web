Modoi on your Mac

Modoi is built to keep your work context on your computer:

  • Screen activity is processed on your Mac by a local model (~2 GB).
  • AI runs on your computer. It even works without Wi‑Fi. Nothing is sent to Claude, OpenAI, or the like.
  • Nothing leaves the computer except optional calendar sync with Google or Outlook when you connect those services.
  • Offline use works without Wi‑Fi; online access is needed only for that calendar sync.

Compared with many productivity apps, Modoi does not send your screen activity to our servers or to third-party model APIs for core features. Product-specific details for Nuclear Blocker (Screen Time, iCloud sync, and Mac filtering) are in the Nuclear Blocker Privacy Policy.
