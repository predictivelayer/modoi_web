Nuclear Blocker

Self-control for Mac and iPhone/iPad. Block chosen sites system-wide in every browser, then stay locked until the session ends.

App Store — coming soon Suggest a feature

Blocking

Blocks chosen sites across Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, and every other browser. Enter youtube.com and the app blocks the whole site, not just the homepage: www.youtube.com, m.youtube.com, youtu.be, and deep links such as https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o6jQo3-iCao. Manual sessions default to 12 hours; max 24.

Strict mode

You cannot cancel once locked. On Mac, quit and uninstall stay hardened while a session runs. The lock survives restart and login.

Schedules

Set recurring weekday windows, including overnight. An active window uses the same strict lock as a manual session.

Cross-device

Mac and iOS apps. iCloud syncs your block list, active session, and schedules.

Always on

Starts at login on Mac and phone. During strict sessions and schedule windows, a watchdog and quit lock stay up.
