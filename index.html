FAQ

Just ask us

Does Modoi work without Wi-Fi or internet?

No. Modoi works without Wi‑Fi. Calendar sync with Google or Outlook needs a connection.

How does Modoi track what I'm working on?

Window titles, app and tab names, plus what is on screen. Raw images are dropped after on-device reading.

Does Modoi access my camera without permission?

Only if you switch it on. Coarse presence signals, then the frame is dropped. Off until you allow it.

Will Modoi interrupt me while I'm working?

One line over the off-task window — only when the screen stops matching what you said you were on.

Can I review or edit time entries Modoi generates automatically?

Every block arrives pending. Approve, reject, rename, or retarget a ticket. Nothing counts until you approve.

Can my employer or manager see my time tracked in Modoi?

No. Time stays on your machine — Modoi doesn’t report hours to your manager.
