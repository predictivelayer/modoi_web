Before · 01

Your rim color shows how present you are

Modoi reads how present you are and colors the edge of your screen. No check-in, no typing what you are on. The rim shifts on its own.

  • Pink when your attention is split.
  • Yellow when you have wandered off track.
  • Green when you are present; blue when you are in flow.

Presence at the edge of the screen

During · 02

Distraction blocked

When the screen stops matching what you said, Modoi says one sentence over the window you drifted into. No red, no lock, no streak to break.

  • It names the drift and the cost: five minutes, gone.
  • It reads your load too, and tells you when to stop rather than push.
  • Type back into the same line to answer it. Then it goes away.

After · 03

Auto-generate event details.

The block already has a name, a time, a Linear ticket, and a project. You can still change them.

  • Each block lands on a Linear ticket (ENG-288, MKT-12).
  • Open it if the ticket or project is wrong.

FAQ

Skip the FAQ. Tell us.

Do I have to start a timer?

No. There is nothing to start and nothing to stop. Modoi asks what you are on, then measures the rest of the day against that.

What does it actually read?

Window titles, app names and tab names, plus what is on screen. The screen is read on your Mac with Apple Vision, and the raw image is dropped straight after.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Raw screenshots and camera frames do not. The text pulled out of them is sent up to be labelled, which is how a window becomes a task. You can turn capture off at any time.

Does it use my camera?

Only if you switch it on. It reads coarse signals such as whether you are still at the desk, then drops the frame. It is off until you allow it.

Will it interrupt me?

One line, over the window you drifted into, and only when the screen stops matching what you said you were on. No lock screens, no sounds, no streaks to protect.

Can I fix what it got wrong?

Every block arrives pending. Approve it, reject it, rename it, or point it at a different ticket. Nothing counts toward your hours until you approve it.

Does the time land on my tickets?

Link a block to a Linear ticket and the hours sit with the work. You are not copying numbers into a second tool at the end of the week.

What does it cost?

Nothing while Modoi is in beta. No card and no account. We will tell you well before that changes.
